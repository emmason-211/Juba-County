About Juba County

Welcome to the official website of Juba County. Juba is the capital and largest city of South Sudan. It is also the capital of Central Equatoria State and serves as the administrative headquarters for Juba County.

Our mission is to provide efficient, transparent, and accountable governance to the people of Juba County, fostering sustainable development, peace, and unity among all communities.

Our Vision

A prosperous, peaceful, and united Juba County with a high standard of living for all its citizens.

Office of the Commissioner

The Commissioner is the chief executive officer of the county, responsible for the implementation of state and national policies at the local level. The office oversees all administrative, developmental, and security matters within the county boundaries.
